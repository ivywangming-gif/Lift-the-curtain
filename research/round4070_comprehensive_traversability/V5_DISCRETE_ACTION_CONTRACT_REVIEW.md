## Discrete geometric separation argument

For an actual enabled fixed wall with x bounds [a,b], use its open 5 mm eroded interior I=(a+0.005,b-0.005). A 40 mm wall has interior width 30 mm. Let x0 be the actually recorded entry witness position, require x0 <= I.low, and let a successful endpoint imply xN > I.low. If every adjacent actual step has |x[k]-x[k-1]| <= 20 mm < 30 mm, take the first k with x[k] > I.low. Then x[k-1] <= I.low and x[k] <= I.low+20 mm < I.high. Thus an actual simulator state lies strictly in the wall's 5 mm interior. Neither monotone forward travel nor interpolation is assumed.

The tube must also lie strictly inside the wall's y/z eroded interior. A witness independently established at least 6 mm inside an exact robot solid makes that state incompatible with verified nonpenetration. The strict step-bound inequality is essential: a step equal to the entire eroded width could connect its two excluded endpoints. The successful endpoint need not exceed I.high; an endpoint inside I already produces the contradiction.

For the most distant D04 wall, x bounds are candidate center + [0.08, 0.12] m and I.low is center + 0.085 m. Existing whole-body clearance and the 6 mm witness radius imply witness xN >= center + 0.106 m, which is already beyond I.low. D01–D03 have nearer low bounds. Actual per-trial wall placement, initial witness state, enabled/filter state and fixedness still require evidence; scene names are not proofs.

This is a statement about the actual discrete 200 Hz PhysX state sequence. It is not a continuous-time guarantee and does not certify a real robot between measured samples. A missing sample, unsupported collision representation, invalid initial witness state, guard violation or unresolved overlap cannot be silently converted to a negative label. Actual demonstrated penetration invalidates physics. Conservative-envelope overlap is only NOT_VERIFIED.

## Evidence and storage

Every actual physics step, including the initialization step before the original E loop's `sim_step=0`, must be accounted for. A separate pre-step reset snapshot, absolute executed-step count, initial/final clock anchors, and per-call phase ledger prevent truncated histories and zero-step phases from masquerading as complete recovery. A zero-step phase retains a zero count and cannot rename the preceding sample. Model observations remain the original legal 50 Hz probe history.

The evaluation-only history uses 500-step compressed NPZ blocks with an immutable SHA-256 index. Each block stores actual timestamps, step, phase and phase instance, actual body link positions/quaternions, cloth positions and cloth velocities. The final phase marker can update only the still-buffered final real sample, never an already finalized block. Admission must verify every block hash, the index hash, continuous actual step/clock coverage, the execution ledger and exact first/final anchors.

For 825 cloth nodes, 60 simulated seconds at 200 Hz require approximately 113 MiB each for float32 node positions and velocities plus 25 MiB body poses, before compression and string/clock overhead. At 180 seconds this is approximately 755 MiB. The largest 1,617-node scene at 180 seconds is approximately 1.37 GiB before compression. These are storage estimates, not measured compression promises; 500-step buffers bound resident history memory. The serial queue's disk admission limit can stop collection below the trial budget and must be reported honestly.

Native geometry has two explicitly different evidence modes. EXACT_NATIVE uses verified primitives or internally consistent actual native convex faces. NATIVE_VERTEX_CONSERVATIVE_ENVELOPE uses the complete actual native vertices' outer hull only to certify sufficient separation and a conservative minimum-x bound; overlap does not prove penetration. The witness can only use EXACT_NATIVE. Each trial must match the frozen native library's asset layers, PhysX binding, actual body order/native inventory, local transforms and ArticulationData implementation. Native geometry and all audit truth remain excluded from deployment features.
